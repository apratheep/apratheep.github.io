Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Pratheep (apratheep@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Hermes accesses the owner's Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs and Contacts) solely to carry out tasks the owner requests, such as summarizing email and adding calendar reminders.

Hermes runs on the owner's own computer. To complete a task, relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only for processing that request. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared for any other purpose.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
